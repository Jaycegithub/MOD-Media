# MOD Media analytics report: key facts

Source: "MOD Media Ads – Analytics Reporting" PDF, 5 pages, from the old site
(http://judy.onlinedigitalprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MOD_Media_Ads_-_Analytics_Reporting.pdf). Read on 27 Nov 2026.

- **Reporting platform:** Mobilytics.
- **What counts as an impression:** a unique cellphone within 50 yards of the truck.
- **What the report shows:** impressions by city, drive time per city, total drive time per region, hourly impressions, and a GPS map of where the truck drove. Daily impressions are reported on monthly contracts.
- **Example campaign:** Truck 1 (Asset 3517), based in Royse City, TX, ran Dec 19, 2024 – Jan 16, 2025.
  - Average daily OTS (opportunities to see): 23,582.
  - Total period OTS: 542,375.
- **Where the truck drove:** the map and charts cover Arlington, Dallas, Fate, Garland, Heath, Hunt County, Rockwall, Rockwall County, Rowlett, Royse City and Wylie. Fort Worth isn't covered.
- **Hours:** impressions are recorded from about 9 am to 6 pm.
- **Company facts:**
  - Legal name: MOD Media Ads, LLC. Family-owned, established in 2022 in Rockwall, TX.
  - Values: trust, integrity and personalized service.
  - Tagline: "With Mod Media Ads, you're not just seen – you're remembered."
  - Contact: (469) 279-2511 · Info@ModMediaAds.com · 181 County Line Road, Rockwall, TX 75032.
- **Note:** the PDF uses "routes" throughout. The new site doesn't, per Jamie's instruction.

## Jamie's answers (28 Nov 2026)
- **Reports:** not automatic with every campaign. They're available when the client asks.
- **Example result:** don't publish the Dec 2024 – Jan 2025 figure. Results vary a lot: some campaigns get 100,000 a day, others about a quarter of that.
- **Screens:** 1920×1080 on each side, 1200×1080 on the rear.
- **Hours:** a one-day campaign is 8 hours of activation.
- **Prices:** none on the website, because they vary too much.
- **Fort Worth and Arlington:** both served, each about 15–20 minutes from Dallas.
- **Outside DFW:**
  - There's no minimum campaign length.
  - Prices drop with more days, more trucks, and so on.
  - Travel fees can apply depending on distance.
